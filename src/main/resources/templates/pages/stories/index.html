• Cauliflower seed production

    Cauliflower is one of the major vegetables grown intensively by farmers in Mylliem C&RD Block. Seed production of the local varieties had been declining because of low temperatures during the vegetative stage, and farmers found it hard to procure seed. With KVK support, a self-help group in Sadew village began producing its own seed in a low-cost polyhouse.

  • Quality protein maize cultivation

    Maize is the second most important cereal in Meghalaya, with an average yield of 1.5 tonnes per hectare in 2010-11. In Dangar, a village on the Indo-Bangladesh border in Mawsynram C&RD Block, the local variety gave yields well below the national average and poor-quality protein. The KVK introduced hybrid quality protein maize through on-farm trials.

  • Maize and French bean intercropping

    Intercropping maize with legumes such as soybean is a traditional practice in the district, but intercropping French bean in maize had not been popularised, and yields were low. The KVK worked with Smti. Shopsimai, a progressive farmer in Laitjem village, to introduce French bean intercropping with a proper package of practices.

  • Kisan Mela cum training programme, January 2014

    A three-day kisan mela on integrated livelihood opportunities for small and marginal farmers, attended by 1,700 farmers from the eight blocks of East Khasi Hills.
