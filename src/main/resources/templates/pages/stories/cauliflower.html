Cauliflower is one of the major vegetables grown intensively by farmers in the Mylliem C&RD Block of East Khasi Hills. The local variety is in high demand, and the farmers of Sadew village have been supplying it to the local market. Over the years, however, seed production of these local varieties declined because of low temperatures during the vegetative stage, and farmers found it difficult to procure seed.

After assessing the problems in the field, KVK officials met progressive farmers in the village and motivated them to form a self-help group, "Iabeitlang SHG", with 10 members. Under its president, Smti. Skodora Kharkrang, a progressive farmer, the group functioned effectively. A few months later the KVK provided training and method demonstrations on seed production technology in cauliflower, and a low-cost polyhouse funded by the Agricultural Technology Management Agency (ATMA) at a cost of ₹1.70 lakh. Each farmer contributed ₹1,110 towards inputs and labour for the entire production process.

With a contribution of ₹1,110 each, the farmers earned three times their investment in the first year, giving them an alternative source of income. The members of Iabeitlang SHG also act as resource persons for farmer-to-farmer dissemination of the technology through this demonstration unit.
