Thank you for visiting the website of Krishi Vigyan Kendra, East Khasi Hills, and for reviewing our privacy policy.

Information we collect

This website does not collect personal information such as your name or address when you visit it, and it does not create individual profiles for commercial marketing.

Like all web servers, our server records technical information about each request — the date and time, the page requested, your IP address and your browser type — for security and to diagnose problems. This information is not used to identify individual visitors, except where required by law (for example, to investigate an attack on the website).

Visitor counter

The visitor count shown at the foot of each page counts each visitor once per day. We do not store your IP address for this: it is combined with the date and a secret key into a code that cannot be traced back to you, and these codes are deleted every night.

Information you choose to give us

If you send us feedback through the feedback form, we collect your name, location, email address and message. We use them only to respond to your message and to provide the information you have requested. Your message is emailed to the KVK and a copy is kept on our server for one year, after which it is deleted automatically. We share it with another Government agency only if your question relates to that agency, or as otherwise required by law. Please do not include personal information that is not needed for your query.

Cookies and preferences

This website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics. The feedback form uses one essential session cookie, which protects the form against misuse; it is deleted when you close your browser or after 30 minutes of inactivity. No other page sets a cookie.

When you first visit, a banner at the bottom of the page asks whether you accept, reject or want to customise optional cookies. Nothing optional is switched on unless you choose it. This website does not currently use any optional cookies. Your choice is stored only in your own browser, and you can change it at any time with Cookie preferences in the page footer.

If you change the accessibility controls (such as text size or contrast), your choices are stored only in your own browser, so they apply on your next visit; they are never sent to us. You can clear them at any time with Reset all in the accessibility controls, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and data is stored on Government servers in India.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, please contact us.
