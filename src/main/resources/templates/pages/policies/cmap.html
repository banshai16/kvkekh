This policy sets out how content is contributed, checked and approved before it is published on the website of Krishi Vigyan Kendra, East Khasi Hills.

Contribution

Content may be contributed by the Senior Scientist and Head, the Subject Matter Specialists and other KVK staff for their areas of work. Contributions are sent to the Web Content Manager in an accessible format (text, or accessible PDF for documents) with captions and descriptions for any photographs.

Moderation

The Web Content Manager checks each contribution for accuracy, spelling, accessibility (headings, link text, alternative text for images) and compliance with the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and the Digital Brand Identity Manual, and prepares it for publication.

Approval

Content is published only after approval by the Senior Scientist and Head of the KVK, or an officer designated by them. Content relating to the Department of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare is approved by the Department before publication.

Contact

The Web Information Manager and Web Content Manager are listed on the Contact Us page.
