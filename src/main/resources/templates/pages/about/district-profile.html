East Khasi Hills is an administrative district in the state of Meghalaya, with its headquarters at Shillong. The district occupies an area of 2,752 km² and has a population of 8,24,059 (Census 2011), making it the most populous district of Meghalaya.

The climate of the district ranges from temperate in the plateau region to warmer tropical and sub-tropical pockets in the northern and southern regions. The whole district is influenced by the south-west monsoon, which generally begins in May and continues until September. The weather is humid for most of the year, except for a relatively dry spell usually between December and March.
