Priority thrust areas of the KVK, in order of rank:

  1. Introduction and popularisation of high-yielding varieties and upgraded breeds: paddy, maize, soybean, tomato, pea, French bean, cabbage, cauliflower, chilli, ginger, plum, poultry, piggery and cattle.
  2. Resource conservation technologies — agronomic practices such as conservation tillage, mulching, contour cultivation and cover cropping.
  3. Integrated pest and disease management in paddy and vegetable crops.
  4. Introduction of improved packages of practices.
  5. Livestock management with appropriate feeding, breeding and health management of poultry, cattle and goatery.
  6. Integrated farming systems.
  7. Soil health and fertility management.
  8. Post-harvest technology of horticultural crops.
  9. Nursery management.
