The objectives of Krishi Vigyan Kendra, East Khasi Hills are:

  1. Planning and conducting surveys of the operational area to prepare a resource inventory, with special reference to identifying the training needs of the farming community.
  2. Planning and conducting production-oriented, need-based short and long duration training courses, both on campus and in the villages, for various target groups, with priority on the poor and weaker sections of society.
  3. Developing and organising non-formal educational programmes such as field days, farm visits, farmers' fairs, radio talks and Farm Science Clubs, as follow-up information support to the training courses.
  4. Organising Farm Science Clubs, both in rural schools and in villages, to induce in the younger generation a liking for and an interest in agricultural and allied sciences and scientific farming through supervised projects.
  5. Providing the practical facilities of the Kendra to teachers and students of higher secondary schools in vocational agriculture.
  6. Developing and maintaining the campus farms and demonstration units on scientific lines, as facilities for providing work experience to the trainees and for disseminating the latest technical know-how.
  7. Imparting general education to rural illiterates and school drop-outs, to make them not only good farmers but also better citizens.
  8. Providing added training facilities in home making and nutrition education for the rural community.
  9. Gradually enlarging the training facilities to encompass other important areas such as home crafts and cottage industries, consistent with the requirements of Integrated Rural Development, in collaboration with the organisations concerned.
  10. Implementing all such schemes of the ICAR and other related organisations which intend to strengthen the training programmes of the Kendra.
