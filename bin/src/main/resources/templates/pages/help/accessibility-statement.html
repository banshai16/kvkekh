We want everyone who visits this website to be able to use it, irrespective of the device, technology or ability. The website is designed to meet the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0) and the World Wide Web Consortium's Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, and follows the Digital Brand Identity Manual (DBIM 3.0) of the Government of India.

What we have done

  • The website works on desktop and laptop computers, tablets and mobile phones.
  • Every page can be navigated with the keyboard alone, and a "Skip to main content" link is provided.
  • Pages are structured with headings, landmarks and lists so that screen readers can navigate them.
  • Images have text alternatives, and photographs in the gallery have captions.
  • Text and background colours meet the WCAG contrast requirements.
  • Accessibility controls let you change text size, contrast and colours (see Help).
  • Links to documents state the file format and size.

Known limitations

  • The following documents are scanned images without a text layer, so screen readers cannot read them and site search cannot find words inside them: the leaflets on cabbage, cauliflower and potato cultivation, and on integrated disease and pest management in rice; and the newsletters for January 2010 to June 2012. We plan to replace them with accessible versions. Some other older PDF documents are not fully tagged for screen readers.
  • If you need the content of any document in another format, please contact us and we will provide it.
  • Websites we link to are maintained by other organisations, who are responsible for their accessibility.

Feedback

If you find any part of this website difficult to use, please tell us through the feedback form or by contacting us.
