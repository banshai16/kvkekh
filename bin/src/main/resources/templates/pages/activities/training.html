The KVK conducts training for farmers, farm women, rural youth and extension personnel in the following areas.

Agronomy

  • Production technology for kharif cereals (paddy, maize)
  • Oilseeds (groundnut, soybean) and pulses (blackgram, cowpea)
  • Organic farming through vermicomposting and green manuring
  • Intercropping (groundnut + soybean, maize + soybean, maize + cowpea) and double cropping of rice

Horticulture

  • Production technology of rhizomatous crops (turmeric, ginger)
  • Nursery raising for winter vegetables
  • Protected cultivation of vegetables and flowers
  • Value addition of crops such as tomato, capsicum, broccoli and cauliflower through early varieties
  • Production of low volume, high value crops
  • Off-season vegetables
  • Exotic vegetable production
  • Production of vegetables with export potential
  • Pruning
  • Cultivation of fruit crops
  • Rejuvenation of old orchards
  • Cultivation of fruits with export potential
  • Plant propagation techniques

Ornamental plants

  • Nursery management

Plantation crops

  • Production and management technology (tuber crops and spices)

Medicinal and aromatic plants

  • Integrated nutrient management
  • Micronutrient deficiency in crops

Livestock production and management

  • Dairy management
  • Poultry management
  • Piggery management
  • Disease management
  • Feed management

Plant protection

  • Controlling soft rot of ginger through integrated disease management
  • Safe use of fungicides and insecticides
  • Integrated pest management
  • Disease management
  • Bio-control of pests and diseases
  • Production of bio-control agents and bio-pesticides

Fisheries

  • Integrated fish farming

Production of inputs at site

  • Seed production
  • Vermicompost production

Capacity building and group dynamics

  • Leadership development in villages
  • Entrepreneurial development of farmers and youth

Rural youth

  • Protected cultivation of vegetable crops
  • Repair and maintenance of farm machinery and implements
  • Nursery management of horticultural crops
  • Training and pruning of orchards
  • Piggery
  • Poultry production
