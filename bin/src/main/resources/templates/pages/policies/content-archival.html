Content on this website is reviewed regularly. Content that is no longer current is moved to the archive rather than deleted, so that it remains available for reference. Each archived item shows the date it was archived.

When content is archived

  • Notices, advertisements and recruitment results: once the notice period, application date or event has passed.
  • Event announcements: after the event, unless they are kept as a success story or in the photo gallery.
  • Other documents: when they are replaced by a newer version, or are no longer relevant.

How long archived content is kept

Archived content remains available on the website for at least three years after it is archived, after which it may be removed with the approval of the Senior Scientist and Head. Annual reports, newsletters and extension publications are kept permanently.
