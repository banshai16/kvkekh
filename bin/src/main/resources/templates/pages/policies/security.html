This website is designed and operated to protect its content and the information of its visitors.

  • The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • The website has no user accounts or logins. The only information it collects is what visitors choose to send through the feedback form, which is protected against automated misuse and kept as described in the privacy policy.
  • The website software is kept up to date and checked regularly for known vulnerabilities.
  • The website is audited by a CERT-In empanelled auditor before launch and after major changes, and any issues found are fixed before the website is used.
  • Unauthorised attempts to upload or change information, or to otherwise cause damage, are strictly prohibited and may be punishable under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

To report a security problem with this website, please contact us.
