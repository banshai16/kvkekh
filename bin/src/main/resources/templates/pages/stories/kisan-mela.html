Kisan Mela cum Training Programme organised under the Tribal Sub Area Plan scheme, 27–29 January 2014.

Krishi Vigyan Kendra East Khasi Hills and the Agricultural Technology Management Agency (ATMA), East Khasi Hills, in collaboration with the Central Potato Research Institute, Shimla, organised a three-day Kisan Mela cum training programme at the Meghalaya State Agricultural Marketing Board Farmers' Market at Sanmer, Upper Shillong, from 27 to 29 January 2014. The theme of the mela was "Integrated livelihood opportunities for small and marginal farmers". It showcased the rich and diverse agri-horticultural products and livestock of the district, and provided an opportunity to demonstrate and exhibit technologies, diagnose and solve farmers' problems, and share the latest scientific information and marketing awareness of agricultural inputs and products.

Central and State Government departments, line agencies and banks showcased their activities and interacted with farmers about their problems and services. Members of NGOs, self-help groups and farmers' interest groups displayed and marketed their products. Free soil testing was offered: at least 500 samples from the district were tested and the reports distributed during the mela itself. In all, 1,700 farmers from the eight blocks of East Khasi Hills registered.

In the training programme on potato cultivation, farmers benefited from updated knowledge and practical sessions by resource persons from CPRS and KVK East Khasi Hills. Farmers welcomed the platform to display their produce, and many asked for the mela to be made an annual event. Prizes were given in several categories for exhibited vegetables, fruits, flowers and other produce. Two leaflets published by the KVK — "Ka rukom rep Motor" (package of practices for pea) and "System of Rice Intensification" — were released, as was a power tiller for custom hiring belonging to Iaijyrngam Cooperative Society, Mylliem.

Shri P.B.O. Warjri, IAS, Additional Chief Secretary, Government of Meghalaya, inaugurated the Kisan Mela cum Training Programme on 27 January 2014 at a function held at the State Biological Control Laboratory, Upper Shillong, in the presence of Shri S. Goyal, IAS, Deputy Commissioner, East Khasi Hills District; Smti. D. Syiemiong, Director of Agriculture, Meghalaya; and Dr A.K. Gogoi, Zonal Project Director, ZPD Zone III, ICAR-RC-NEH. Senior officers from ICAR and the State agriculture and allied departments, village heads and farmers attended, along with Dr R.J. Rabindra, Dean, College of Post Graduate Studies, Barapani; Shri N.S. Nongbri, Director, MAMETI; and Dr N.K. Pandey, Head, Division of Social Sciences, Central Potato Research Institute, Shimla.
